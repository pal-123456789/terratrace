# 15 — Competition Readiness and Compliance

## Current official event finding

The official 2026 NASA Space Apps event page lists **November 14–15, 2026**. Submission opens November 14 at 9:00 a.m. and closes November 15 at 11:59 p.m. local time. Official local-event pre-event guidance says participants may prepare and explore tools/data, but may not begin developing the solution intended for submission before the event begins.

Accordingly, October–November 13 work must be readiness only. The competition project repository, challenge-derived calculations, UI, rankings, and demo are created after kickoff.

### Rules evidence packet

Capture and archive:

- official event page;
- exact challenge page/brief;
- participant terms and conditions;
- local-event no-predevelopment guidance;
- judging criteria;
- team and submission requirements;
- exact local timezone rule;
- authenticated dashboard state if available;
- retrieval timestamp and screenshot/PDF/HTML.

Do not treat search snippets, old-year PDFs, local blog paraphrases, or editable project portals as authoritative when official terms exist.

## 2026 judging dimensions

The official local-event page found lists:

- Impact;
- Creativity;
- Validity;
- Relevance;
- Presentation.

For the exact definitions and applicable global-selection process, archive the live participant/judging materials. Do not assert that local guidance automatically reproduces every global judging detail.

## Open-source/citation obligations

Official participant terms identify requirements around original content, use of materials the participant has rights to, source citations, and open-source/public availability. Before release:

- choose an OSI-compliant license for original code;
- preserve source-specific license/terms separately;
- cite all input datasets, papers, imagery, and external assets;
- do not assume source data can be redistributed merely because it is publicly downloadable;
- include contributor/license acknowledgements;
- verify AI-generated content/code is compatible with event terms and platform rules.

## Clean-room policy

Before kickoff, permitted readiness examples should use unrelated synthetic data and generic tools. Do not create:

- a TerraTrace repository intended for submission;
- challenge-specific Mars/Earth comparison results;
- challenge-specific derived features/rankings;
- UI/prototype built around the selected challenge;
- a submission video, challenge screenshots, or prepared solution assets;
- a project-specific data bundle.

May prepare:

- a blank repo template with generic config only if official guidance allows it;
- generic tests on synthetic surfaces unrelated to the challenge;
- a data-source inspection checklist;
- generic geospatial/CRS practice;
- team roles and event schedule;
- a claim firewall and release checklist;
- a clean-room runbook.

When in doubt, ask the organizer in writing and preserve the response.

## Submission checklist

- Project exists in official submission portal.
- Challenge selected and title/description aligned.
- Source/credit citations included.
- Demo link loads without private auth.
- Video and screenshots match submitted build.
- License and code link are correct.
- No claim of NASA endorsement.
- No unauthorized data or assets.
- Submit early enough to verify receipt.
- Save confirmation and final timestamp.
