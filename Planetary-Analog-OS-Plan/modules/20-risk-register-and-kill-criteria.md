## Kill criteria

Stop the current feature path when:

1. A critical claim cannot be assigned C3/R4 evidence.
2. A comparison is unstable and the UI cannot show the instability honestly.
3. A source cannot be lawfully used or cited.
4. The result depends on unsupported physical scale.
5. The user concludes mission/rover readiness from the output.
6. The product cannot produce an evidence trace.
7. The product cannot run offline from pinned assets.
8. A site’s rights/community/ecology status is represented as positive without evidence.
9. Pre-event work risks violating official competition terms.
10. Validation is cut in favor of cosmetic scope.

## Pivot ladder

```text
Full morphology comparison
  ↓ if support/rights fail
Transfer Audit with no ranking
  ↓ if data still fails
Evidence/metadata provenance demonstrator
  ↓ if challenge path fails
Generic planetary data literacy prototype for post-event research
```

Each pivot is an honest reduction in claim maturity, not a quiet rebranding of failure.

## Red-team questions

- What source or test would disprove this result?
- Is the body/region/task scope narrower than the language?
- Is the feature physically observable at its declared support?
- Does the ranking change because of a user requirement or an artifact?
- Is an “unknown” being treated as “safe” or “available”?
- Could a reviewer reproduce the displayed number?
- Would a NASA scientist reject the interpretation?
- Would a local community reject the site representation?
- Would a buyer pay for the workflow or only admire the demo?
- Is the long-term moat evidence, governance, or only UI rhetoric?
