# 13 — Market, Procurement, and Value

## Market truth

The initial buyer is not “NASA” as an abstract market. NASA and other agencies contain programs, laboratories, contracting vehicles, procurement rules, technical reviewers, and budget owners. TerraTrace must first prove a repeatable workflow to a narrow user group.

## Customer segments

| Segment | Pain hypothesis | First proof | Likely path |
|---|---|---|---|
| University rover lab | Reproducibility and test-site planning | Study package reused by another team | Research pilot/grant |
| Planetary analog program | Site/test evidence fragmented | Protocol registry and outcomes | Pilot/procurement/partnership |
| Space agency concept team | Early assumption audit and traceability | Reviewable study package | Research contract or framework |
| Robotics company | Test condition selection and evidence | ROI/time saved + integration | Paid pilot/enterprise |
| Geospatial/remote-sensing lab | Cross-body support and provenance | Benchmark/workflow adoption | Research partnership |
| Site steward | Governed visibility and study requests | Consent/access workflow | Partnership/service |
| Educator/public | Understand analog limits | Accessible prepared study | Open product/public funding |

## Product tiers

### Open research core

- Public schemas and methodology.
- Static study package.
- Reproducible benchmark.
- Public docs and fixtures.

Purpose: trust and ecosystem formation.

### Research workspace

- Private study packages.
- Team review and versioning.
- Controlled sources and site metadata.
- Exportable evidence reports.
- Protocol/outcome linkage.

### Institutional platform

- SSO/RBAC.
- Audit logs.
- Private data planes.
- Integration APIs.
- Governance workflows.
- Support and service levels.

### Network services

- Governed site registry.
- Authorized field partners.
- Protocol/campaign coordination.
- Outcome evidence and benchmarks.
- Certification/review only if an independent governance body exists; do not self-certify mission readiness.

| Institutional subscription | Teams pay for private/reviewable study workflows | Repeated usage and budget owner |
| Paid research pilot | Custom task/source integration | Repeatable delivery and margin |
| Evidence/benchmark license | Organizations pay for curated validated cases | Unique outcome data and rights |
| Field-study services | Governed protocol/site coordination | Operational capability and liability model |
| API/integration | Robotics/GIS tools pay for evidence-compatible outputs | Technical integration and retention |
| Training/education | Institutions pay for curricula/workshops | Adoption and learning outcomes |

Do not put pricing in the competition pitch until customer interviews support a range. Pricing hypotheses can be tested after release.

## Procurement realities

Agency procurement may require:

- approved vendor/contract vehicle;
- information security and data handling;
- accessibility compliance;
- export/control screening where relevant;
- source and license traceability;
- insurance/liability for field services;
- budget cycle and statement of work;
- technical evaluation and pilot acceptance;
- public-interest and ethics review.

The software should be exportable as a study package so procurement does not lock evidence inside a hosted vendor.

## Market validation sequence

1. 10–15 problem interviews across labs, analog programs, geospatial researchers, and site stewards.
2. Give each a non-sales study package and ask them to audit it.
3. Measure missing evidence and workflow friction.
4. Offer one narrowly bounded pilot with clear success metric.
5. Charge only when a buyer confirms budget and acceptance criteria.
6. Track repeat use, not compliments.

## Honest scale ladder

### Millions

Possible annual revenue after validated institutional pilots and repeatable research workflows. Not an immediate result.

### Billions

Requires the product to become a standard workflow/data layer across planetary robotics, field autonomy, analog programs, and associated extreme-environment testing. This is highly conditional.

### Trillions

No credible basis exists for a company valuation claim. At most, PAOS could become an open standard whose evidence influences very large future exploration/robotics/infrastructure expenditures. Strategic influence is not revenue, valuation, or ownership of all downstream value.

## Unit-economics questions

Before scaling, measure:

- cost to onboard a source/product;
- cost per study compute/storage;
- human scientific review hours;
- governance/rights review hours;
- customer time saved;
- study-package reuse rate;
- gross margin by product tier;
- support burden;
- retention/repeat studies;
- outcome evidence produced per customer.

## Customer kill criteria

Pivot the commercial thesis if:

- users only want one-off consulting and do not reuse structured evidence;
- no buyer can identify a budget owner;
- data/legal/governance work costs more than customer value;
- the product saves no measurable time/risk;
- agencies require a nonviable procurement posture;
- site operators do not accept the governance model.
