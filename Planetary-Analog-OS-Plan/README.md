# Planetary Analog OS — Master Plan Library

## Purpose

This is the **full-system strategy and execution library** for building Planetary Analog OS: an evidence infrastructure that connects planetary mission requirements to Earth test environments, explicitly models transfer gaps, and accumulates validated field/simulation outcomes.

This library separates three truths:

| Horizon | What it is | What it must prove |
|---|---|---|
| **Competition nucleus** | TerraTrace v0.1, Mars-only map-scale morphology transfer audit | Transparent, reproducible comparison; no false rover-validation claims |
| **Institutional platform** | Mission-conditioned analog study and evidence workspace | Repeated users, expert review, study outcomes, governance, procurement fit |
| **Planetary infrastructure** | Cross-agency evidence and standards network | Validated transfer records, interoperability, trusted governance, compounding data advantage |

The long-term economic potential is **conditional**, not guaranteed. A billion/trillion-dollar company is not created by a map or by asserting a huge TAM. It would require a trusted standard, validated outcomes, institutional adoption, and a network that becomes harder to replace as evidence accumulates.

## Global non-negotiables

1. **No false equivalence.** Earth is never labeled “the Moon” or “Mars.”
2. **No opaque universal score.** Morphology, transfer gaps, and field feasibility remain separate.
3. **No hidden resolution error.** Every feature declares spatial support and limits.
4. **No live dependency in the canonical demo.** Pinned artifacts are mandatory.
5. **No pre-event submitted-solution development.** Follow the official 2026 event terms and clean-room rule.
6. **No invented value.** Revenue, valuation, strategic throughput, scientific value, and public benefit are different claims.
7. **Unknown is a result.** Tie, blocked, insufficient evidence, and not comparable are successful states.
8. **No field operation without authorization.** Data availability is not access permission.
9. **Every visible number needs lineage.** Source → transform → feature → result.
10. **The first release is a nucleus, not the whole OS.** Long-term ambition must not inflate short-term claims.

## Source integrity

Use the current official sources and archive exact pages/products before making claims. The event status and rules were found through the official Space Apps pages, while the scientific source families include NASA/PDS MOLA, USGS 3DEP, NASA Earthdata, and STAC. Search results are leads; product-level claims require product labels, terms, checksums, and transformation records.

## Definition of done for the plan library

The library is complete when an implementation team can answer, without re-planning:

- What is the product and its scientific boundary?
- What is the exact canonical dataset and why is it lawful/usable?
- What equations and algorithms generate each displayed result?
- What evidence graph links every claim?
- What is built now versus later?
- How is it tested, falsified, and red-teamed?
- How does it become a business or public infrastructure layer without hype?
- What gates stop work when science, rights, schedule, or claims fail?
- What files and artifacts must exist at every milestone?

No module may override the global claim firewall or compliance rules.
