PLANETARY ANALOG OS / TERRATRACE v0.1

Map-scale morphology transfer audit

A reproducible comparison of a Mars reference terrain unit with candidate Earth terrain units at a declared common support of 1000.0 m.

Scope. This screens terrain morphology only. It does not estimate rover traversability, validate autonomy, establish geological equivalence, certify site access, or certify mission readiness.
Result: TIE — Tie - evidence does not separate candidates. No single unit is recommended; see the result state and sensitivity below.

MORPHOLOGY COMPARISON (normalized distance to reference)

Decision axes (extended): slope, relief, ruggedness, connectivity, aspect_dispersion, hypsometric_integral. Lower is closer. A star marks a recommended follow-up unit. No single composite "Mars-likeness" score is produced; ranking is by Pareto dominance, and a tie is a valid result.

WHAT THIS COMPARISON CANNOT ESTABLISH

  • T0 topographic_morphology — Map-scale slope/relief/ruggedness comparable at declared support. Do not infer rock-scale obstacles or wheel-scale hazards.
  • T1 low_slope_connectivity — Broad corridor structure is comparable at declared support. Not a traversable route or path guarantee.
  • T2 gravity — Mars surface gravity (~0.38 g) is not reproduced on Earth. Do not infer mobility, slip, or stability transfer.
  • T2 atmosphere_pressure — Mars atmospheric density/pressure is not reproduced. Do not infer dust lofting, aero-thermal, or EDL behaviour.
  • T3 regolith_mechanics — Mechanical soil behaviour is not inferred from a DEM. Do not claim traction, sinkage, or bearing equivalence.
  • T2 radiation — Surface radiation environment is not reproduced. Do not infer radiation effects from terrain.
  • T3 thermal_cycle — Diurnal/seasonal thermal regime is not inferred from terrain. Do not infer thermal survival.
  • T3 illumination — Illumination geometry is context only unless modelled. Do not infer power or imaging performance.
  • T3 communications — Relay/line-of-sight geometry is not inferred from terrain. Do not infer link availability.
  • T4 site_access — Field access/permission is not implied by public data. Do not present as an authorized test site.
  • T4 ecology_community — Ecological and community governance must be verified. Do not plan field work without authorization.
